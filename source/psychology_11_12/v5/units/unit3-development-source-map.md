## Accuracy boundaries

- Developmental influences are interactive and contextual; avoid one-cause/destiny language.
- Milestones are broad developmental expectations, not individual diagnostic deadlines.
- Attachment classifications are relationship-specific behavioural patterns, not diagnoses or fixed personality types; cultural caregiving context matters.
- Parenting findings are not presented as simple one-way causes.
- Adolescent development is not reduced to a single immature brain region or one maturity timeline.
- Emerging adulthood is context-dependent rather than universal.
- Fluid/crystallized changes are population-level patterns with substantial individual variation.
- Normative aging is distinct from pathology/dementia.
- Death and dying (OpenStax 9.4) remains optional/enrichment and is not graded.

## Student-safety / disclosure boundary

No Unit 3 task requires students to disclose pregnancy, parenting, family history, attachment style, relationships, identity, diagnoses, or private developmental experiences. The assessed Rowan case is fictional.
